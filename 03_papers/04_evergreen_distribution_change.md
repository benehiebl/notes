# Title:
Distribution changes of evergreen broad-leaved species in Italy

> [!abstract] Motivation
> - Spread of evergreens into deciduous forests, with documented dieback of Q. ilex in other areas
> - lack of spatially explicit quantification

> [!question] What research questions will be answered?
> - What are hotspots of change and trajectory patterns?
> - What are potential drivers of change?
> - Where are trajectories moving and what are future habitats?


> [!info] What will be done?
> - statistical analysis of 40 years of annual LTC and DTS maps regarding the trajectories of evergreens
> - correlation analysis of changes towards environmental (temperature, drought frequency) and anthropogenic (land-use) factors
> - future prediction of patterns (Hidden Markov?) using trajectories and climate(/land-use) drivers

> [!success] What are the expected results?
> A comprehensive understanding of change patterns in evergreen broad-leaved vegetation:
> - future maps of distribution
> - gain and dieback zones
> - understanding main drivers of forest change


## Introduction
- Spread and expansion of evergreens + its drivers (milder winters etc.) in Italy, southern alps and other regions
- Dieback and increased mortality of evergreens in Italy and other regions due a combination of drought and infections
- Understanding the interplay of regional differences, climatic drivers, and change rates/trajectories for comprehensive forest understanding
