# Title: A multi-sensor fusion approach for estimating forest climatic stress

> [!abstract] Motivation

> [!question] What research questions will be answered?
> - Can drought, temperature and photosynthetic activity proxies from remote sensing be used to estimate vegetation stress?
> - Which type of vegetation is more affected (grassland, shrubs, forest)?

> [!info] What will be done?
> - Combining several proxies into 3 stress indicators:
> 	- Water deficit: swir, NDMI, VPD, pr weekly, SAR
> 	- temperature: LST, tasmin, tasmax
> 	- photosynthetic activity: NDVI, EVI
> - data sources:
> 	- Landsat: LST, NDMI, NDVI, EVI, swir
> 	- Sentinel-2: NDMI, NDVI, EVI, swir
> 	- MODIS: LST, NDMI, NDVI, EVI
> 	- ERA5: VPD, PR_weekly, tasmin, tasmax, wind speed
> 	- CHELSA: VPD, PR_weekly, tasmin, tasmax, wind speed
> - Kalman filter for each stress indicator, combined in a global filter for 3D trajectory estimation
> - ? Combining proxies into single indicator unclear?

> [!success] What are the expected results?
> - drought or temperature proxy extremes leads ultimately to a decrease in photosynthetic activity
> - main cause finally infestation or extreme event -> dieback or decrease in productivity
