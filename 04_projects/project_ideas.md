# Project ideas

Brainstorming general ideas for research projects


# Forests under pressure

> [!abstract] Motivation
> - climate change threatens current forest structure through increasing temperatures, droughts and extreme weather events
> - these stress factors favor pest infestation and tree die-back

>[!info] Research gap
>- spatio-temporal mapping of stress-factors and relation to die-back
>- real-time monitoring of stress state of forests

> [!question] Research questions/Hypothesis
> - Which forests are most affected?
> - How are stress patterns evolving over time?
> - How can we monitor critical situations in real-time?


# Dying trees: climatic drivers and spatio-temporal dynamics


> [!abstract] Motivation
> - Tyrolean pine forest stands face die-back of single trees
> - Similar patterns in spruce-dominated forests
> - potential cause: drought, temperature extremes

>[!info] Research gap
>- climatic drivers of die-back unknown
>- spatio-temporal mapping difficult due to lack of high resolution imagery
>- scale and future trajectory unknown

> [!question] Research questions/Hypothesis
> - what events trigger die-back?
> - Which trees are affected and why?
> - What are future forecasts?
